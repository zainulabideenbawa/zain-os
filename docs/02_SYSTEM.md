# Zain OS — The System (background)
*v4 · Cycle 1: Mon Sep 28 → Sun Dec 20, 2026 (12 weeks) · replaces v3.1*

> **Five salah. Five checkpoints. One streak.**
> Your day already has five fixed moments that Allah set for you. The whole system hangs on them. You don't need to remember a routine; you only need to answer one small question after each prayer.

---

## Part 1 — The whole system on one page

### The 5 checkpoints

| Checkpoint | After… | 🔥 Minimum (keeps the streak) | ⭐ Target (makes it a great day) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌅 **Fajr** | Fajr | Qur'an 1 page · move 20 min | Jamaat · Qur'an 15 min · adhkar · run/gym |
| ☀️ **Dhuhr** | Dhuhr | **Big Rock done:** 90 min deep work on the WIG | 3 hrs deep work · 3 revenue actions |
| 🌤 **Asr** | Asr | **Arabic 10 min** | Arabic 20 min · Deep Work 3 done |
| 🌇 **Maghrib** | Maghrib | — (just the salah) | Phone away at dinner · family time |
| 🌙 **Isha** | Isha | **Muhasaba + plan tomorrow** (5 min) | Read 20 min · in bed by 21:45 |

Across all five: **every salah prayed on time** is part of the minimum. Every checkpoint is one tap.

**Day kept 🔥** = all 5 salah + Qur'an + move + Big Rock + Arabic + muhasaba/plan.
It's the same six minimums as v3.1, arranged so the day reminds you of them itself.

### Only three numbers matter

1. **🔥 Streak.** Your daily chain.
2. **⏱ Deep-work hours this week.** Your WIG engine.
3. **📊 Weekly score.** % of targets hit. A win is ≥ 70% in weeks 1–2, then ≥ 85%.

Everything else is detail you can look at on Sunday.

### The cycle

**WIG (confirm):** 5 Apps launched & earning.
- **Keep-alive:** TekScrum revenue.
- **Personal project (Maker Blocks):** Friday 14:30–16:30 + Saturday 11:30–13:15 (~4 hrs/week). One project per cycle, picked from the Parked list. SolSniper upkeep takes the first 30 min of Friday's block when it needs it.
- **Parked:** everything else, until Dec 20.

## Part 2 — The coach panel: what each would change in v3.1

I ran v3.1 past each framework and asked what that coach would say. **The biggest finding: v3.1 was right, but too heavy to run.** It had 6 minimums, 4 pillars of targets, 7 pillar streaks, points, freezes and a weekday template of 20+ lines. That's a lot to hold in your head at 5 AM. v4 keeps the substance and cuts the load.

| Coach | Their critique of v3.1 | What v4 does |
|---|---|---|
| **BJ Fogg** (*Tiny Habits*) | "Too many new behaviors at once. Each needs a clear prompt and an instant celebration." | Each habit is prompted by a salah · targets phase in over 4 weeks · a **celebration** after every tap (haptic + flame grows) |
| **James Clear** (*Atomic Habits*) | "Make it obvious. Habits need visible cues and identity, not willpower." | One-time **environment setup** (Part 5) · every check-in is a *"vote for who you're becoming"* · never miss twice |
| **Nir Eyal** (*Hooked*, *Indistractable*) | "You need a complete hook loop, and pacts that stop you escaping." | Full hook loop (Part 3) · three pacts: **effort** (Focus mode), **price** (sadaqah), **identity** (your niyyah statement) |
| **Duolingo** (the best streak system built) | "The streak *is* the product. Protect it, dramatize it, celebrate milestones, and don't spam." | Streak front and centre · **milestones** · "streak at risk" drama · freezes · **one well-timed reminder**, not ten |
| **Alex Hormozi** | "Too many metrics. Track the few inputs that drive the outcome." | **Three numbers only** · revenue actions + deep-work hours are the inputs |
| **David Goggins** (*Can't Hurt Me*) | "Where's the accountability mirror? Where's the cookie jar for hard days?" | **Mirror note** (WIG + why on your bathroom mirror) · **Cookie Jar**: your past wins, shown when you slip |
| **Jocko Willink** (*Discipline Equals Freedom*) | "Don't negotiate with yourself at 5 AM. Decide the night before. Setback? *Good.*" | The plan is locked the night before · the alarm is across the room · a missed day shows **"Good. Comeback today."** |
| **Tony Robbins** | "State before strategy. Connect to purpose before you act." | **Why Card** each morning: niyyah + identity + cycle countdown · RPM = WIG (result), niyyah (purpose), top 3 (action plan) |
| **Mel Robbins** (*5 Second Rule*) | "Hesitation kills. Count down and move." | **5-4-3-2-1 → Start** button that begins a 5-minute timer on the Big Rock |
| **Cal Newport** (*Deep Work*) | "One daily shutdown ritual; fixed end time; cap deep work." | Isha muhasaba = the shutdown · 4-hr cap · in bed by 21:45 |
| **Katy Milkman** (*How to Change*) | "Plan for the bad day. Reward the comeback. Use fresh starts." | **Bad Day Mode** · comeback badge · resets on Monday / month / Islamic month |
| **Islamic tradition** | "Consistency even if small. Muhasaba before and after action. Renew intention." | The minimums are small by design · morning niyyah + evening muhasaba · *"Do not belittle any good deed"* (Muslim) |

## Part 3 — The hook loop (why you'll open it every day)

```
TRIGGER  → the salah itself (+ one push after jamaat)
ACTION   → one tap, under 10 seconds
REWARD   → flame grows, milestone badges, a new coach line, an ayah/hadith card on a kept day
INVEST   → tonight's plan sets tomorrow's first action; the longer the streak, the more you protect it
```

After about 2 months (roughly how long habits take to form), the salah becomes the trigger by itself. You'll finish Asr and reach for your Arabic notebook without the app.

## Part 4 — Reminders: few, perfectly timed

The rule: **one reminder per checkpoint, plus a safety net.** It's never more than 8 a day, so you don't start ignoring them.

| Time | Reminder |
|---|---|
| 21:30 (Tahajjud eves) | `Tahajjud tonight — in bed now` |
| Jamaat − 15 min (×5) | `Asr jamaat in 15 · after: Arabic 10 min · 🔥 12` |
| 07:30 | `Big Rock: {tomorrow's first action from last night}. 5-4-3-2-1 → Start` |
| 07:45 if the timer isn't running | `Big Rock hasn't started. Just 5 minutes?` |
| After Isha + 25 min | `Muhasaba + plan — 5 min · 🔥 12 on the line` |
| 21:45 if not done | `Streak at risk. 3 minutes saves it.` |
| Next morning after a miss | `Good. Comeback day — keep today and the streak is restored.` |

**Silence rules:**
- While a focus timer is running, only prayer reminders come through.
- After 21:45, nothing except the Tahajjud alarm.
- If you ignore the same reminder 5 days running, the coach asks whether to move it or change it, instead of nagging.

## Part 5 — Motivation stack (what carries you on low days)

| Lever | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Streak + milestones** | Badges at 3 · 7 · 14 · 21 · **40** · 66 · 100 days. The 40-day and 66-day marks get a special card. |
| **Loss aversion** | The streak turns amber when at risk. The same number is on the reminder. |
| **Comeback** | A kept day after a miss restores the streak and earns a Comeback badge (never miss twice). |
| **Freezes ❄️** | You earn 1 per 7 kept days (bank up to 2). Use them for travel or illness. |
| **Why Card** (morning) | Your niyyah · identity line · *"Week 1 of 12 — 84 days to launch 5 apps"* |
| **Cookie Jar** | Your "went well" lines from muhasaba, saved automatically. Shown when you miss or open the app on a bad day. |
| **Coach** | A short, varied reply after each muhasaba. It names one win, one barrier and one fix. |
| **Partner** | Scorecard shared every Sunday + a 15-min call. |
| **Stakes** (optional, from week 3) | Sadaqah pledge per lost week. |
| **Hard Mode** (optional) | One stretch challenge a week, e.g. extra Tahajjud, a 10k run or a 6-hr build day. |

**Bad Day Mode** (one button):
- Today is minimums only. Targets are hidden and there's no guilt.
- Reminders are reduced.
- The Cookie Jar opens.

This is how you keep the streak on sick days, travel days and heavy client days.

**Identity statement** (edit to your words):
*"I am a Muslim who keeps his promises to Allah and to himself, and a builder who ships every day."*

## Part 8 — Evening muhasaba + plan (the keystone, 5 min)

1. **Tick:** the app pre-fills whatever you already tapped today. You just confirm.
2. **Muhasaba:** energy 1–5 · one line on what went well (→ Cookie Jar) · one line on what didn't, and why (forgot / procrastinated / tired / overcommitted / distracted) · one line of shukr or istighfar.
3. **Plan:** top 3 (#1 = the WIG) · **the exact first action for the Big Rock** · one if-then for the likely obstacle · check tomorrow's meetings and Tahajjud night.
4. **Close:** phone out of the room → read 20 min → sleep.

**Morning:** the Why Card + tonight's plan → tap confirm. One minute.

Prayer times are calculated for Karachi (University of Karachi method, Hanafi Asr) for Sep 28. The app recalculates them daily. Adjust the jamaat times to your masjid.

**Weekday (Mon–Thu)**

```
04:45  Tahajjud (set nights)          05:05  Wake → masjid
05:30  FAJR → Qur'an + adhkar → run (Mon/Wed) / gym (Tue/Thu)
07:15  Breakfast · Why Card + confirm plan

10:00  Deep Work 2 (1.5 hrs, WIG)
11:30  TekScrum ops + meetings + revenue actions

~17:00 ASR → Arabic 10–20 min → family / walk
18:22  MAGHRIB → dinner, phone away
~20:15 ISHA → muhasaba + plan → read → sleep 21:45
```

**Friday:** run → Big Rock → Jumu'ah + Surah Kahf → lunch → **Maker Block 14:30–16:30** (SolSniper check first if needed, then your personal project) → Asr → Arabic → evening off.
**Saturday:** gym → Arabic class 9–11 (counts as both Big Rock and Arabic) → break → **Maker Block 11:30–13:15** → Dhuhr → afternoon off, family.

**Maker Block rules:**
- It's a weekly target (≥ 3 hrs), not a daily minimum, so it never threatens the streak.
- It never takes weekday Big Rock time. Weekday evenings and Sunday stay protected.
- Friday's block is always yours. Saturday's block goes to the WIG only if last week was lost.
- Give each parked idea a home: write the next step for the personal project at the end of each block, so it stops nagging you during the week.
**Sunday:** weekly review 09:00 (30 min) → partner check-in → rest, family.
**Tahajjud:** Sun/Wed/Fri pre-dawn · target 3 a week, minimum 1.

**TekScrum meetings** (all inside 11:30–13:15):

## Part 10 — Honest verdict

No system is perfect, and no app can do the salah or the deep work for you. What makes this one different from June:

1. **It's built around moments you already never skip,** your five prayers.
2. **Each step is tiny** and prompted at the right moment.
3. **A miss can't spiral:** there's never-miss-twice, freezes and Bad Day Mode.
4. **Someone sees the score,** every Sunday.

If you only protect two things, protect the **Isha muhasaba** and **bed by 21:45**. Everything else follows from them.

## Part 12 — The barakah layer (from *The Productive Muslim*, Mohammed Faris)

Productivity = focus × energy × time, and barakah multiplies all three. The book looks at it through three lenses: spiritual, physical and social. The social lens and the barakah-bringing practices were the gaps. **None of these adds a daily minimum.**

| Addition | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Dua after Fajr: *Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an, wa rizqan tayyiban, wa 'amalan mutaqabbalan* (Ibn Mājah 925) | Victory Hour, before the morning adhkar |
| **Bismillah** at every Big Rock start | Shown on the Start button |
| **Daily sadaqah**, small and automated ("Charity does not decrease wealth", Muslim 2588) | Daily target, separate from the weekly stake |
| **Khushu rating** 1–3 in muhasaba (salah as focus training) | Muhasaba; the weekly review shows your weakest prayer |
| **Energy heatmap**: one tap after each focus block (low/okay/high) | After 2 weeks, the Big Rock moves to your real peak time if 07:30 isn't it |

| **Sunnah fast**, Monday or Thursday (al-Tirmidhī 747) | Optional Hard Mode from week 5 |

## Part 13 — Monthly outcome scorecard (lag measures)

The daily and weekly numbers track **what you do**. This scorecard checks **whether it's working**. It's the first 10 minutes of the monthly check.

**Dates:**
- **Baseline: Sun Oct 4**
- Sun Nov 1
- Sun Dec 6
- Sun Dec 20 (cycle review)

**Setting targets:** set each Dec 20 target on Oct 4 from your real baseline. Targets change only at a monthly check.

| Area | Outcome | Measured | Suggested Dec 20 target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build (WIG) | Apps live | Manual | 5 |
| Build (WIG) | App revenue this month | Manual (store / Stripe) | First paying users on ≥ 1 app; amount set Oct 4 |
| Business | TekScrum revenue this month + deals closed | Manual (accounts / CRM) | Set Oct 4 |
| Body | 5k run time | Manual (timed 5k on the first run day of each month) | ~10% faster than baseline |
| Body | Weight or waist | Manual (same scale/tape, morning) | Direction set Oct 4 |
| Deen | Qur'an pages · Tahajjud nights · salah on time % | **Auto** from checkpoint taps | ≥ 120 pages/month · ≥ 10 Tahajjud/month · 100% on time |
| Arabic | Qur'anic words known | Manual (vocab deck/notebook count) | ~+300 words by Dec 20 |
| Growth + social | Books finished · roles-check weeks green | Auto + a tap when you finish a book | 3 books · ≥ 3 of 4 weeks green |

**How to read it (lead vs lag):**
- **Effort high, outcomes moving:** keep going. Consider a 30-day upgrade.
- **Effort high, outcomes flat for 2 months:** the strategy is wrong. Change the daily work (e.g. distribution instead of features). Discuss it with your partner, and pray istikhara if it's a big change.
- **Effort low, outcomes flat:** an execution problem. Shrink the minimum that keeps breaking and check sleep. Don't lower the target yet.
- **Effort low, outcomes moving:** something else is driving it. Don't let it hide a slipping routine.

**Day 1 checklist addition:** record baseline outcomes and set the Dec 20 targets by Sun Oct 4.
