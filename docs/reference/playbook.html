The day at a glance

The sun moves. You answer five small questions.

Each salah is a checkpoint. After it, the app asks for one or two tiny things, and each one is a single tap. Do the minimums and the day is kept, even on your worst day. Hit the targets and it's a great day.

Fajrالفجر
~05:30 jamaat

Minimum: Qur'an 1 page · move 20 min

Target: jamaat, Qur'an 15 min, adhkar, run or gym

Dhuhrالظهر
~13:15 jamaat

Minimum: Big Rock done, 90 min deep work on the WIG

Target: 3–4 hrs deep work, 3 revenue actions

Asrالعصر
~17:00 jamaat

Minimum: Arabic 10 min

Target: Arabic 20 min, Deep Work 3 done

Maghribالمغرب
~18:25 jamaat

Minimum: the salah

Target: family dinner, phone in another room

Ishaالعشاء
~20:15 jamaat

Minimum: muhasaba + plan tomorrow, 5 min

Target: read 20 min, in bed by 21:45

Azan times are for Karachi on Sep 28 (University of Karachi method, Hanafi Asr). Jamaat times are approximate: set your masjid's times once and the app recalculates every day as the seasons shift. Day kept = all 5 salah on time + every minimum above.

The operating loop · al-Ghazali, Iḥyāʾ, Book of Murāqaba & Muḥāsaba

A 900-year-old system for self-discipline, run every day.

Al-Ghazali describes six stations for disciplining the self. They map almost exactly onto modern habit science: commit in the morning, watch through the day, review at night, apply a consequence, push harder, and learn from the week. This is the backbone of Zain OS.

1 · Morningمشارطة

Mushāraṭa

Set terms with the self

After Fajr: niyyah, identity line, confirm last night's plan. You decide the day before the day decides you.

2 · All dayمراقبة

Murāqaba

Watchfulness

The five salah checkpoints. One tap each keeps you aware of the plan without having to remember it.

3 · After Ishaمحاسبة

Muḥāsaba

Self-accounting

A 5-minute review: what went well, what didn't and why, shukr or istighfar. Then plan tomorrow.

4 · Weeklyمعاقبة

Muʿāqaba

Consequence

A self-chosen stake: a sadaqah pledge for a lost week, confirmed by your partner. Skipping costs something, and the cost does good.

5 · When laggingمجاهدة

Mujāhada

Striving

Hard Mode: one stretch challenge a week. When you fall behind, you push harder instead of quitting.

6 · Sundayمعاتبة

Muʿātaba

Honest self-reproach

The Sunday review and the accountability mirror. You say the truth about the week, then fix the system, not just the mood.

"Hold yourselves to account before you are held to account."

Identity, niyyah, and the goal hierarchy

Motivation starts at the top of the ladder.

Grit research (Duckworth) shows that people who persist connect their daily tasks to a top-level purpose. Atomic Habits adds that every action is a vote for who you are becoming. So the daily minimums are tied to the highest thing you care about.

Top · Why

The pleasure of Allah. To be the strong believer: strong in faith, body, knowledge and provision.

Middle · Who

A Muslim who understands the Qur'an in Arabic, a fit and disciplined body, a builder who ships products, and a provider for his family and his team.

Daily · What

Five salah, Qur'an, Arabic, move, Big Rock, muhasaba. Six small votes a day.

Identity line · read each morning

"I am a Muslim who keeps his promises to Allah and to himself, and a builder who ships every day."

Edit it into your own words. It appears on the Why Card every morning, and the coach uses it when you slip.

The Victory Hour · 5AM Club × Miracle Morning × Sunnah

You already wake at five. Now make that hour count.

Robin Sharma's 20/20/20 formula (Move, Reflect, Grow) and Hal Elrod's SAVERS both say that whoever owns the first hour owns the day. Fajr gives you that hour for free, and it's the time the Prophet ﷺ asked Allah to bless.

The streak · Clear × Duolingo × Milkman

A chain that survives bad days.

Streaks motivate, but research shows a broken streak makes people quit (Silverman & Barasch). So this chain bends instead of snapping. You miss once, you come back, and the chain lives.

Never miss twice

One miss turns the chain amber; it doesn't reset. Keep the next day and it's restored with a Comeback badge. The comeback is the most-rewarded moment in the system (Milkman's megastudy: rewarding a return after a missed workout was one of the strongest interventions).

Freezes

You earn one freeze for every 7 kept days and can bank up to 2. Use them for travel, illness or a family emergency. Goals with a few emergency passes hold up better than all-or-nothing goals.

Bad Day Mode

One button. Targets hide, reminders soften, the Cookie Jar opens. You still do the minimums, the streak survives, and there's no guilt. This is how the chain lasts through flu, travel and heavy client days.

Phase-in (Fogg: start tiny, then grow)

Week 1 — minimums only; win = 6 of 7 days kept · Week 2 — adds the 3-hr deep-work target; win at 70% · Weeks 3–12 — all targets; win at 85% (12 Week Year) · Shrink rule — if something breaks the chain twice in 14 days, make that minimum smaller (e.g. Big Rock 90 → 60 min) and grow it back once it's held for 14 days.

The hook loop · Nir Eyal, Hooked

Why you'll open it every day without trying.

Habit-forming products run one loop: something prompts you, you take one small action, you get a reward, and you put something in that sets up the next prompt. Here the prompt is the salah itself. After about two months, you'll finish Asr and reach for your Arabic notebook without the app.

1 · Trigger

The adhan

One push after each jamaat names the next tiny thing: "Asr done · Arabic 10 min · 🔥 12".

2 · Action

One tap

Under 10 seconds. A 5-4-3-2-1 → Start button for the Big Rock (Mel Robbins). The minimum is always 2 minutes or less to begin (Clear).

3 · Variable reward

Alhamdulillah

A haptic tap and the flame grows (Fogg's "celebrate"). Sometimes a milestone, sometimes a coach line, sometimes an ayah card on a kept day.

4 · Investment

Tonight's plan

The evening plan writes tomorrow's first action into the 07:30 reminder. The longer the chain, the more you'll protect it.

The motivation stack

What carries you on the days you don't feel like it.

Motivation rises and falls (Fogg). So the system stacks several levers, and on any given day at least one of them works.

Meaning · Pink, Covey

Why Card

Every morning: your niyyah, identity line and "Week 1 of 12 · 84 days to launch 5 apps."

Momentum · Hardy, Clear

The streak

The flame and its milestones. The number is printed on every reminder, so you know what's on the line.

Evidence · Goggins

Cookie Jar

Every "went well" line from muhasaba is saved. When you slip, the app opens it and shows you proof of who you've been.

Honesty · Goggins

Accountability mirror

A sticky note on your bathroom mirror with the WIG and one line of why. You see it at Fajr wudu and before bed.

Coaching · AI + Milkman

The coach

A short, varied reply after each muhasaba: one win, the barrier behind a miss, one fix for tomorrow. Tone: Gentle, Direct or Jocko.

Witness · 4DX, 12WY

Your partner

Every Sunday: share your scorecard and have a 15-minute call. Progress that others see is much more likely to happen (Harkin meta-analysis).

Stakes · Eyal, Milkman

Sadaqah pledge

Optional, from week 3: a lost week means sadaqah, confirmed by your partner. It works as three pacts: effort (Focus mode), price (sadaqah) and identity (your line).

Challenge · Goggins 40% rule

Hard Mode

One opt-in stretch a week: an extra Tahajjud, a 10k run, a 6-hour build day. You're capable of more than you feel, and this proves it gently.

Fresh starts · Milkman

Reset points

Every Monday, 1st of the month and new Islamic month shows a clean-slate card. Falling off always has an obvious way back in.

The barakah layer · The Productive Muslim, Mohammed Faris

Focus × energy × time. Barakah multiplies all three.

Faris's book looks at productivity through three lenses: spiritual, physical and social. Zain OS was already strong on the first two. These additions fill the gaps, mostly the social lens and the practices that bring barakah. None of them adds a daily minimum, so the streak stays exactly as simple as before.

Spiritual · after Fajr

Ask for barakah by name

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا

After the salam at Fajr: "O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, good provision and accepted deeds" (Ibn Mājah 925). Every Big Rock starts with Bismillah, shown on the Start button.

Spiritual · daily

Daily sadaqah

A small amount every day, automated so it never depends on memory. "Charity does not decrease wealth" (Muslim 2588). It's a daily target, and separate from the weekly sadaqah stake.

Spiritual · focus training

Khushu rating

Your salah is five attention-training sessions a day. Muhasaba gets one 1–3 tap for khushu, and the weekly review shows which prayer is weakest so you can work on it.

Physical · energy

Energy heatmap

One tap after each focus block (low, okay, high). After two weeks the app shows when you're actually sharp, and the Big Rock moves to your real peak if 07:30 isn't it.

Physical · food

The one-third rule

"A third for food, a third for drink, a third for air" (al-Tirmidhī 2380). A light lunch before qailulah protects Deep Work 3 from the post-lunch slump. This is what "clean eating day" means here.

Physical · optional

Sunnah fast

From week 5, one Monday or Thursday fast a week as a Hard Mode option (al-Tirmidhī 747). On fast days, keep the workout light and the Big Rock early.

Social · weekly

Minimum in every role

A Sunday roles check: family time most evenings, a call or visit to parents and kin, one act of service at the masjid or in the community, and one thank-you to someone on the team. No role goes to zero.

Social · decisions

Istikhara + shura

For big decisions (a new WIG, a hire, a large spend or a new project): pray istikhara, consult one trusted person, and sleep on it. This is built into the Dec 20 cycle review.

Growth · monthly

30-day upgrades

From week 5, each month adds one upgrade challenge: for example Tahajjud three nights a week, then the Monday/Thursday fast, then a Qur'an target. One upgrade at a time, never three.

Your daily and weekly numbers track what you do. Once a month you check what actually changed. If the effort is high and these numbers don't move, the plan is wrong, not you. Eight numbers, one per area. The app fills in the ones marked auto from your logs; you type in the rest.

Effort high · outcomes moving

Keep going

The plan works. Don't change anything. Consider a 30-day upgrade.

Effort high · outcomes flat (2 months)

Change the strategy

You're doing the work, but it's the wrong work. Change what you do each day (e.g. more distribution instead of more features for the apps). Discuss it with your partner and pray istikhara if it's a big change.

Effort low · outcomes flat

Fix the system, not the goal

This is an execution problem. Shrink the minimum that keeps breaking, check your sleep, and look at what the barrier was in muhasaba. Don't lower the target yet.

Effort low · outcomes moving

Look closer

Something else is driving the result (a lucky deal, the team). Enjoy it, but don't let it hide a slipping routine.

The 12 laws of Zain OS

Thirty-one books, boiled down to twelve rules you can remember at 5 AM.

Salah is the schedule.Every habit is anchored to a prayer you already never miss.
Never miss twice.One miss is an accident. Two is the start of a new habit.
Big Rock before messages.No feeds, news or inbox until 90 minutes of deep work are done.
Decide at night, execute at dawn.The morning never gets a vote on whether to start.
Minimum on bad days, target on good days.Consistency beats intensity. The most beloved deeds are the consistent ones.
One goal per cycle.Everything else is parked, not forgotten. It gets its turn on Dec 20.
Track inputs, not feelings.Hours, reps, actions shipped. Feelings follow the numbers.
Make starting stupidly easy.5-4-3-2-1. Just five minutes. The first 2 minutes are the whole battle.
The phone sleeps outside.Out of the bedroom, off the dinner table, silent during the Big Rock.
Celebrate every tap.Alhamdulillah. A habit is wired in by the feeling right after it.
Someone sees the score.Every Sunday, one person you respect sees the truth.
Rest is part of the work.Qailulah, Sunday, and sleep straight after Isha.
